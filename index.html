About Me

Hello, I'm Khaila, Manabat and you may refer me as Zein or Khai.

I'm a student who enjoys making art in my free time. I like drawing, experimenting with different styles, and turning random ideas into something visual.

I'm not exactly an expert. I just enjoy the process of creating something and seeing how it turns out. I use my dream drawing tablet that is a Huion 22 inch plus, although frequently I have been experiencing art block.
